SOURCE AND LICENSE ACCESS

Original application: GPL-3.0-or-later. The source ZIP accompanies the candidate packages. LICENSE contains GPLv3; LICENSE-SCOPE.md describes scope and retained third-party licensing.

The corresponding-source companion contains captured third-party source archives and exact-version/source-location inventories. A link to a repository's moving main branch alone is not treated as complete corresponding source. Runtime/engine components and any unresolved preferred-form sources are explicitly tracked. The release gate blocks publication while required source remains unresolved.

There is no configured public source-download URL yet. A public distributor must provide a stable, working source channel alongside the binary distribution, retain versioned source/build instructions and honor all required redistribution rights. Contact the distributor of your copy for its corresponding source. Do not publish signing keys, passwords, device profiles or certificate private keys.
